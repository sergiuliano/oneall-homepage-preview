Social Login: Joomla Extension

PluginsSocial LoginLink to this post

From the archive. These posts were written between 2011 and 2015 and describe OneAll and the social networks as they were then. For current instructions, see the documentation.

We are proud to announce you that we have released Social Login for Joomla!

OneAll Social Login for Joomla! is a professionally developed and free extension that allows your users to register and login to your Joomla! website with their existing social network account.
It helps increasing your Joomla! user registration rate by simplifying the registration process and provides permission-based social data retrieved from the social network profiles.

OneAll Social Login integrates with your existing Joomla! registration system so you and your users don’t have to start from scratch. Allows your user to login and register on your Joomla! website with social networks like Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Paypal, LiveJournal, Hyves, Google and Yahoo amongst others.
