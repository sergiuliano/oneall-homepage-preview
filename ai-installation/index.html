Install OneAll Social Login on my WordPress website.

Follow the OneAll AI installation guide: https://docs.oneall.com/ai/install.md

- Use the official OneAll Social Login plugin from WordPress.org (oa-social-login). Use WP-CLI if you can reach my server. If not, guide me through Plugins → Add New.
- When you need my OneAll account, I will sign in myself.
- Never show my private API key in this chat. Save it with `npx oneall env pull`, or let me paste it into the settings.
- Ask before you change my live site, DNS records or existing user accounts. Never change my nameservers.
- When you are done, test a real sign-in, run `npx oneall doctor` and tell me what you changed.
Open in Claude Open in ChatGPT Or paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot or Gemini.

Best results: use an assistant that can open your site’s files or your server. A chat app alone can guide you step by step, but you will do the clicks.

What it does

The same steps a developer would take. Done for you.

  1. 1Finds your platformReads your site to see what it runs on and which version, then picks the matching OneAll plugin.
  2. 2Connects your OneAll SiteYou sign in to OneAll. It creates or picks your Site and turns on the networks you want, like Google and Apple.
  3. 3Installs the official pluginAlways the official OneAll plugin when one exists. For your own code, it makes the changes on a separate branch.
  4. 4Stores your keys safelyYour private key goes straight into your site’s settings. It never appears in the chat.
  5. 5Checks your web addressAdds your domain to your OneAll Site. Changes DNS only if you want your own sign-in address, and only after asking.
  6. 6Tests and reports backSigns in once to prove it works, then lists every change it made so you can review or undo it.

Access & safety

It asks first. You stay in charge.

Your assistant works with your permission, on your computer or your server. OneAll never gets access to your site.

  • Your site’s filesYour project folder, or your hosting control panel for a CMS. On your own code, it works on a separate branch.
  • Your OneAll accountYou sign in yourself, in your browser. Your assistant gets limited access you can withdraw at any time.
  • Your site’s admin panelOnly for plugin installs without server access. You type your own password; the assistant never asks for it.
  • Your domain, rarelyOnly for your own sign-in address, like login.yoursite.com. That is one CNAME record. Your nameservers never change.

It always asks before it

  • turns sign-in on for your visitors
  • adds or changes a DNS record
  • changes how existing members sign in
  • installs anything on your server

It never

  • shows your private key in the chat
  • asks for your passwords
  • changes your nameservers
  • matches members by email alone

Questions

Good to know.

Which AI assistants can do this?

Any assistant can follow the prompt. Assistants that can open your site’s files or connect to your server, like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Copilot, can do the whole install. Chat apps without that access will guide you while you click.

Will it need my nameservers?

No. Your nameservers never change. Most installs need no DNS change at all. If you want your own sign-in address, like login.yoursite.com, your assistant shows you one CNAME record and waits for your approval before adding it.

Is my private API key safe?

Yes. The key goes straight from your OneAll account into your site’s settings or .env file. It is never typed into the chat, so it never ends up in your assistant’s history.

What happens to my existing members?

They keep signing in the way they do now. Social sign-in is added next to your current login. Your assistant links a social account to an existing member only when that member confirms it, never by matching email addresses alone.

Can I undo it?

Yes. At the end your assistant lists every change it made. Plugins can be turned off in your admin panel. Changes to your own code are on a separate branch, so you can review them before they go live.

My platform isn’t listed. Now what?

Choose My own code. Your assistant adds OneAll directly, using the JavaScript library and the OneAll API. That works with any website or language.
